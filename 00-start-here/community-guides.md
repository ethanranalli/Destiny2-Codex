Useful conclusions for this helper:
- A nominal flat ability refund and a passive cooldown are different mechanisms. Check energy scalars before claiming a loop is self-sustaining.
- In DIM, select intended subclass, exotic, and mods before assessing stat feasibility. Its optimization can assume upgrades; those assumptions must be disclosed.
- Use per-tab status and actual test conditions. A sheet can contain newer weapon data alongside stale or explicitly ignored build tabs.
- Community authors and popularity rankings are discovery aids. A complete build must still pass local legality and feasibility checks.

These points are applied in [build framework](../builds/buildcrafting-framework.md), [validation](../builds/build-validation.md), and [damage reasoning](../builds/damage-and-survival.md).

## Admission rule for new resources

Keep a source when it adds a distinct job, useful original evidence, or a good fallback. Record author, URL, topic, checked date, update date if visible, and limits. Prefer original tests/maps over copied listicles. Do not add an inaccessible candidate as a validated guide, and do not ingest unrelated games from multi-game sites.
