# Lore, Fashion, Accessibility, and Destiny Support

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. These are Destiny 2 topic routes.

## Lore and story

Use [Ishtar Collective](https://www.ishtar-collective.net/) for original entries/transcripts and [Destinypedia](https://www.destinypedia.com/) for discovery and cross-references. Distinguish explicit source text, character claims, interpretation, and speculation. Prefer a concise explanation unless a deep lore discussion is requested. Spoilers are allowed.

For chronological story, distinguish playable campaigns from vaulted story and narrated summaries. Quote briefly and link the original entry.

## Fashion and collection

Identify class, desired theme, owned ornaments/shaders, and any fixed exotic. Recommend exact items with obtainable alternatives. An ornament changes appearance; it does not automatically change set-bonus membership or exotic function.

Check Collections, [Light.gg](https://www.light.gg/), [Braytech](https://bray.tech/), and the actual vendor for acquisition. Distinguish an old item listing from a current sale. State Bright Dust/Silver costs only after a live check.

## Settings, accessibility, and troubleshooting

For Destiny-specific settings or problems, begin with the exact symptom/error, platform, timing, and what changed. Use [Bungie Help](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us), [server status](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049199271-Destiny-Server-and-Update-Status), and [known issues](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/39007140765204-Destiny-2-Known-Issues-and-Vital-Information).

Give the smallest relevant diagnosis and reversible next step. Separate service outages, account/ownership, API issues, network errors, and local performance. Do not generalize a Steam-only report to the user's Epic installation.

Settings advice is guidance by default. Do not change Windows, network, game files, accounts, or security settings without an explicit request. Avoid unsupported anti-cheat workarounds or speculative optimization scripts.

For accessibility/input, explain the relevant in-game option and how to compare results. Do not assert that a particular visual/audio cue is present without a source or observed UI.
