# Catalysts, Crafting, and Acquisition

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Exact drop sources, currencies, perk pools, and eligibility must be checked for the item in question.

## Exotic/catalyst route

1. Establish exact weapon and whether the user owns it, owns the catalyst, completed its objective, and applied it.
2. Identify present source: quest/mission, encounter drop, ritual reward, vendor, kiosk, or other specific requirement.
3. Verify ownership, difficulty, account/character restrictions, repeatability, and rotation.
4. Give the fastest eligible route and the precise next objective.
5. Compare the catalyst's actual effect against upgrade effort; do not use an old stat-only description by default.

The local [Court infographics](../data/local-resource-index.md) cover June catalyst additions and revisions. They are dated reference leads. For example, [9.7.0.1](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/destiny_update_9_7_0_1) fixed the Shattered Throne final-encounter Wish-Ender catalyst drop and Collective Obligation catalyst progression; older bug reports can misdirect a farm.

## Crafting/enhancement route

Distinguish a craftable pattern, Deepsight progress, enhanced traits on a random roll, and gear-tier upgrading. Check the exact weapon/version before claiming one supports another.

For a crafted or enhanced recommendation include:
- Pattern/weapon access and progress from personal data.
- Legal chosen perks and relevant enhanced benefits.
- Required weapon level, currencies, unlocks, and present cost if relevant.
- Whether tier upgrades change stats/origin benefits and whether selectable perk count changes.
- What improves the actual build and what can wait.

Do not spend materials or reshape gear through an account UI as part of advice. A perfect low-tier roll can be worth upgrading, but verify its path and compare the cost with a repeatable farm.
