# Armor, Stats, Archetypes, and Sets

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Use the local Compendium's Armor Perks and Game Mechanics first, then current evidence for exact breakpoints.

## Current planning rules

Armor 3.0 uses Health, Weapons, Class, Grenade, Melee, and Super. Plan actual points, including fragment changes, rather than old multiples-of-ten tiers. Effects above 100 differ from those below 100 and can differ between PvE and PvP. Check the exact stat curve before assigning a numerical target.

Health is not simply the former Resilience DR rule. Weapons is not a blanket damage bonus to every target. Distinguish additional shield capacity, healing, resistance, cooldown, energy generation, damage, and ammo effects.

The initial six are documented in [Bungie's Armor 3.0 introduction](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/twid_06_26_2025); the later six appear in [9.7.0, Armor Stats](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/destiny_update_9_7_0). That patch spells Demolitionist as "Demolitionst"; use the actual in-game/manifest name when searching or specifying a roll.

Choose the archetype that supports the full target distribution. Higher tier alone does not establish a better equipped build. Check actual total, distribution, mod capacity, tunable stats, set, and upgrade cost.

## Exotic armor and mod capacity

Exotic armor is not selected like a tiered legendary. Read its actual roll and available energy. [9.7.0.1](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/destiny_update_9_7_0_1) distinguishes eligible post-Edge-of-Fate non-Collections exotics for the extra-energy fix; do not assume every Collections or older piece can fit the same mods.

A build must show all five armor slots, exact mods/counts, stat mods, and energy used/available. Check slot restrictions and any artifact discounts. If capacity is unknown, state the minimum and give a fitting fallback.

## Set bonuses

Compare 2+2 and four-piece sets by reliable trigger, activity benefit, and lost stats/mods. Name the set and actual number of equipped pieces. Account for the exotic slot, especially an exotic class item.

Check PvE/PvP limits, target types, cooldowns, ally requirements, and stacking against current Armor Perks entries. A matching ornament is not evidence that a piece contributes to the functional set.

Legacy raid armor can still have value for activity mods. Compare that benefit against modern sets instead of automatically dismantling it.

## Optimize owned armor

1. Use the latest relevant export and keep every piece's identity, slot, class, archetype, set, tier, stats, and upgrade state.
2. Pin the exotic and mandatory activity/set pieces.
3. Apply fragment stat changes, required mods, energy, and ability constraints.
4. Set the survival/engine minimums, then optimize the objective's next most valuable stat.
5. Inspect a feasible combination in DIM/D2ArmorPicker if authorized account access exists. Do not pretend a planner has loaded an export it has not read.
6. Separate permanent totals, conditional bonuses, and aspirational targets.
7. Recommend replacements only after comparing complete combinations.

Keep pieces that enable a distinct useful setup, stat threshold, set, or activity mod. Shard advice needs an actual better replacement or redundant role; raw stat total and rarity are insufficient.

Full builds must report all six stats, their priority order, and realistic assumptions. No owned armor export was present during this audit.
