# Weapons and Roll Verdicts

Reviewed: 2026-09-06.

Answer the exact requested roll first. Use personal inventory to establish ownership; public data to establish legal perks and behavior; current testing to assess performance.

## Evidence route

1. Latest personal roll, all selectable perks, weapon version, catalyst, tier, and mod.
2. Local Compendium entry for exact trait/weapon behavior; refresh changed or uncertain values.
3. D2Foundry or current manifest/Light.gg for perk columns and stats.
4. Endgame Analysis for chase value; Boss Damage for the actual damage role.
5. Relevant patch and known-issue check.

A popularity score or wishlist match is not a measured performance rating.

## Verdict

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| God Roll | Best or near-best legal roll for a named meaningful role under stated conditions |
| Keep | Useful role and no clear owned replacement |
| Test | Promising but uncertain behavior or practical consistency |
| Niche | Specific encounter, strategy, or utility |
| Replace | Usable until a named better option is available |
| Archive | Collection, sentimental, historical, or hard-to-reacquire item |
| Shard | Redundant or ineffective for the user's needs after comparison |

For a general weapon request, separate PvE and PvP rolls, best use, key perks, and keep/replace status. For an explicit PvE-only question, keep the PvP note brief or omit it unless it changes a shard decision. Never dismantle inventory as part of advice.

## What a roll comparison needs

- Exact item/version, slot, damage element, ammo type, frame, tier, origin trait, catalyst.
- Selected barrel, magazine, both main traits, masterwork/stat option, and mod for a god-roll request.
- Legal perk pools and enhancement path; don't merge traits from different reprisals.
- Activation reliability, downtime, reload bypass, reserves, target type, and range.
- Damage inside the actual window for PvE; first-duel consistency and stated target conditions for PvP.
- Which owned weapon already fills that role, what is lost by replacing it, and acquisition cost.

Natural multi-perk drops and a tier-upgraded single-roll weapon are not identical. The [9.7.0 gear rules](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/destiny_update_9_7_0) allow upgrades for supported weapons but do not imply extra selectable traits appear. Verify the item's upgrade path and current cost before recommending a spend.

## Anti-Champion 2.0

Under [Bungie's checked frame rules](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/dev_insights_weapons_artifacts_focusing_preview), weapon effects are intrinsic and do not require the old artifact selection/ADS charge-up. Exact exotic/special frames still need their own entry.

| Common intrinsic frame | Effect |
|---|---|
| Aggressive / High Impact | Unstoppable |
| Precision / Adaptive | Barrier |
| Lightweight / Rapid-Fire | Overload |

Niche examples in that official table include Area Denial breach GL (Overload), Rocket-Assisted sidearm/pulse (Unstoppable), Caster sword (Barrier), Vortex sword (Overload), and Wave sword (Unstoppable). Use the exact weapon entry for all exceptions, multi-mode weapons, and exotic effects.

For a complete build:
- Identify the actual Champion types in the activity.
- State primary weapon/verb, activation, ammo requirement, and reliable fallback.
- Check stun recovery/immunity and source overrides.
- Do not assume every crowd-control effect is a stun, or that every weapon can inherit every additional Champion effect.

## PvE and PvP priorities

PvE: useful damage/utility at the encounter's range, sustainable ammo, realistic perk activation, and support for the build engine. PvP: first-fight value, range/falloff, handling, stability/recoil, accuracy, special economy, and team-shot plan. Mouse input does not make stability irrelevant.

Use [damage reasoning](../builds/damage-and-survival.md) for comparable DPS/TTK conditions. Flag untested breakpoints.

## Compact answer shape

**Weapon — verdict for the stated role.**
Then a small table: current roll, PvE ideal, PvP ideal if relevant, why the key perks matter, replacement, and Champion effect if useful. Finish with one actionable keep/test/farm decision and a short evidence note.
