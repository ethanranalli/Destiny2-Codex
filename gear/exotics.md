# Exotics

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Use the local Compendium's Exotic Weapons, Exotic Armors, and Exotic Class entries for exact behavior.

## Correct slot logic

One exotic weapon and one exotic armor piece can be equipped simultaneously. Compare exotic weapons against other weapons and exotic armor against other armor. They do not compete for one shared slot.

Choose armor for its actual engine: damage, ability returns, control, survival, neutral game, or support. Choose a weapon for its role and performance after accounting for its catalyst, ammo, artifact, and legendary alternatives.

## Exotic class items

Relativism (Hunter), Solipsism (Warlock), and Stoicism (Titan) need:
- The correct class and Prismatic for their perk effects under the checked Compendium.
- Exact first and second Spirit perks from legal columns.
- Each Spirit perk's own definition, which can differ from the full exotic.
- Evidence the roll is owned, or an explicit aspirational label and obtainable alternative.

Do not recommend an unspecified "good class item" as a finished choice. Never substitute one into a pure elemental subclass and assume its perks function.

## Evaluate an exotic

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Trigger, duration, cooldown, restrictions | Prevent a loop that cannot activate |
| Kills versus hits, enemy rank, range, subclass/ability | Establish reliability in the encounter |
| Catalyst state | Can change the weapon's function and ranking |
| Champion effect and mode | Do not transfer behavior between PvE and Guardians |
| Artifact and set interactions | Check stacking, ammo, charge, and energy |
| Patch and known issues | Old build videos can rely on removed behavior |
| Acquisition and exact owned roll | Separate practical setup and future chase |

For damage use the current Boss Damage entry; for viability use a relevant current analysis. The Endgame Analysis tabs labeled "Exotic Armor (ignore)" and "Builds (ignore)" are not current exotic rankings.

## Catalyst priority

Prioritize catalysts that materially change a build's damage, uptime, ammo, survival, control, or utility. Next consider handling/consistency. Collection-only value is lower priority unless requested.

Track owned, obtained, completed, and equipped/applied separately. Do not call a catalyst complete because the weapon is owned or appears in Collections.
