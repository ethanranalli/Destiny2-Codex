# PvP

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Default input: mouse and keyboard on PC.

## Mode first

Trials, Competitive, Iron Banner, casual Crucible, private matches, and rotating modes can have different Power, ammo, ability, and artifact rules. Confirm the exact mode.

The checked [artifact rules](../builds/artifacts.md) disable artifact perks in Trials and Competitive. Other modes need their own check. Do not assume a PvE perk/stat benefit applies to Guardians.

[Monument-era official guidance](https://origin-static01.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/twid_06_11_2026) describes three-map Trials weekends and weapon attunement. Do not describe every weekend as one fixed map and one featured weapon from an older guide. Verify active maps, passage/reward rules, and Iron Banner timing live.

## Build and weapon evaluation

Prioritize:
1. Range band, first-duel consistency, accuracy/recoil, handling, and an effective primary/special pairing.
2. Survival and disengagement that actually change the chosen matchup.
3. Class/ability uptime and realistic Super use.
4. Objective control, revive protection, team-shot lanes, and special-ammo discipline.
5. Kill-chain benefits only after the neutral duel is solved.

Health, Weapons, and Class are candidates, not fixed universal targets. Verify precise stat benefits, damage ramps, overheat behavior, overshields, and TTK before giving breakpoints. See [damage reasoning](../builds/damage-and-survival.md).

## Coaching output

For improvement requests, diagnose a specific loss pattern: missed shots, bad range, wide peeks, isolated fights, bad timing, wasted special, objective neglect, or overcommitment. Give one concrete drill, one habit cue, and a measurable sign of improvement. Do not fabricate performance statistics without a match report or user data.

For a full build use the common complete card; for a roll verdict use [weapons](../gear/weapons.md). Include map/range assumptions, first engagement, disengage/re-engage, special, and Super/objective plan.

Live maps/statistics: [Trials Report](https://destinytrialsreport.com/), current in-game card, and [public source registry](../00-start-here/public-source-registry.md).
