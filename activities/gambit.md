# Gambit

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Gambit mixes combatants and Guardians; distinguish their damage and ability rules.

## Decide the job

| Role | Priorities |
|---|---|
| Collect/clear | Fast waves, safe mote banking, ammo and movement |
| Blocker control | Reliable major damage and bank awareness |
| Invade | Guardian damage at useful range, map/portal timing, limited ammo |
| Defend invasion | Awareness, cover, team information, protecting valuable motes |
| Primeval phase | Current phase/envoy rules, ammo, reliable damage and survival |

For solo queue do not assume teammates cover every role. For duo, assign complementary jobs and include the backup if an invade or bank defense is needed.

Verify current invasion thresholds, ammo rules, Primeval damage gates, and reward/focusing rules for an exact strategy; do not reuse old numbers by habit.

For a build, include the PvE artifact section and state which effects apply to combatants versus Guardians. Check mode-specific restrictions and intrinsic Champion utility only if relevant; do not invent Champions for a standard mode.
