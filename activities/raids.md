# Raids

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Standard raid planning assumes a six-player team. Low-player-count strategies require explicit encounter and feasibility checks.

## Catalog for routing

Last Wish; Garden of Salvation; Deep Stone Crypt; Vault of Glass; Vow of the Disciple; King's Fall; Root of Nightmares; Crota's End; Salvation's Edge; The Desert Perpetual.

This list is not the user's owned content or completed history. Verify present access, difficulty, exact encounter, and loot source. Use [Bungie's activity guide](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/46668136913172--10-Activities) and the [community guide/loot index](../00-start-here/community-guides.md).

## Plan a run

- Establish first clear, challenge, farm, title, flawless, or speed goal.
- Assign mechanics, add control, support/debuff, and damage responsibilities.
- Agree callouts and orientation before starting; don't treat one convention as universal.
- Identify wipe timers, recovery opportunities, revive limits, and damage phase triggers.
- Match weapons and Super to crit access, distance, movement, safety, phase duration, and team buffs.
- Include a practical fallback rotation and an ammo preparation plan.

Use [guide protocol](guide-protocol.md) for every phase and [Boss Damage](../data/live-google-sheets.md) for damage inputs.

## Farming

Check featured status, encounter loot, challenge rewards, difficulty/grade, focusing, crafting/tiering, and repeatability. Distinguish ordinary encounter drops, red borders/pattern progress, hidden chests, exotic chances, and vendor purchases. Do not turn one farmable reward into a claim that all rewards are unlimited.

Exact featured activities must come from the current reset interval. A static table of launch rewards cannot prove today's pool.

For Pantheon, use [its own version](pantheon-and-srl.md). Do not copy original-raid mechanics or damage modifiers without checking.
