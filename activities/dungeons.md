## Solo/duo plan

- Prioritize reliable completion, survival outside ability/kill chains, and total reserves.
- Explain every mechanic the solo player must perform; assign complementary duo roles.
- Identify safe places to regain health, manage adds, or generate ammo before damage.
- Match phase damage and total damage to the boss; do not force unsafe melee/precision rotations.
- Include recovery if a buff expires, a damage phase is missed, or ammo runs low.
- For solo/flawless triumphs, verify uninterrupted-run, checkpoint, fireteam, and death/disconnect rules before starting.
