# General PvE

Reviewed: 2026-09-06.

| Activity goal | Optimize |
|---|---|
| Solo Ops / ordinary farming | Fast, repeatable completion and eligible rewards |
| Fireteam/Arena Ops | Clear roles, objective speed, flexible damage and self-sustain |
| Pinnacle or difficult activities | Actual Champion coverage, safe range, recovery, total damage |
| Campaign / Exotic mission | Mechanics, access, checkpoint rules, encounter-specific loadout |
| Destination / Distortion | Exact objective, combatant rules, reward conditions |
| Score challenge | Required score/grade, modifiers, deaths, and completion time |

## Preparation

Check current difficulty, Power rules, modifiers, elemental shields/threats, equipment lock, matchmaking, and reward conditions. Then cover adds, majors, boss, range, survival, and ammo.

Bring a reliable response to each Champion actually present. Use [weapon/Champion rules](../gear/weapons.md), then verify the exact weapon and ability; do not assume old seasonal artifact counters.

For Banes or unusual enemy affixes, identify the actual affix and its trigger/counter. Use current Compendium/Court material; a generic "tankier enemy" explanation can miss the required response.

Solo builds need survival outside kill chains and sufficient total ammo. Duo builds can divide jobs, but state who covers a stun, debuff, revive, or mechanic; do not silently assume the friend supplies it.

## Answer

Give activity/difficulty assumption, next objective, recommended loadout or build if requested, survival and Champion plan, mechanics/positioning, and reward route. Use [guide protocol](guide-protocol.md) for walkthroughs and [build output](../builds/build-output-template.md) for builds.

For current farming, consult [farming](../progression/farming.md) rather than copying an old playlist reward claim.
