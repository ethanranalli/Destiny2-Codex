# Guide and Walkthrough Protocol

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Use for quests, campaigns, encounters, puzzles, collectibles, exotic missions, activity clears, and challenges.

## Intake

Use the exact activity/quest, current step or encounter, difficulty/version, fireteam size, role, and goal. Infer ordinary details; ask only if the missing detail changes the next action. A player's quoted objective overrides a generic guide's assumed progress.

For returning players or changed Director nodes, verify access and current launch path. For solo/flawless or title requirements, verify continuous-run, checkpoint, death, disconnect, and team restrictions from the exact triumph.

## Research

1. Local relevant note and personal progress.
2. Exact current objective/encounter rules and known issues.
3. One suitable text guide/map or original creator explanation from the [community library](../00-start-here/community-guides.md).
4. Boss Damage/current build sources only if damage optimization is part of the question.

Check whether the source covers original, reprised, Pantheon, normal, Master, challenge, or another variant. Reuse old geometry only when applicable; do not carry old rewards or exploit skips forward without checking.

## Answer shape

Lead with **the next action or objective**.

Then provide, as relevant:
1. Prerequisites: ownership, quest unlocks, gear/mechanic tools, players, difficulty.
2. Where to launch or go: present node, destination, and landmark.
3. Roles and callouts: who does what, orientation, and naming convention.
4. Ordered mechanics: action, visible/audio success cue, and next trigger.
5. Damage phase: how it opens, safe position, duration/stop cue, practical rotation.
6. Failure recovery: common mistake, whether recoverable, and what to do immediately.
7. Completion and rewards: claim point, loot source, repeatability/lockout, next quest step.

For a short "what next" request, give only the next few steps and relevant blockers. For a full guide, cover every phase needed for completion.

## Quality checks

- Define "left/right" and numbered callouts relative to a viewpoint; mention alternate team conventions.
- Distinguish mandatory mechanics from optional chests, collectibles, challenges, and faster routes.
- Do not promise a random drop, unsourced drop rate, or guaranteed clear time.
- Distinguish encounter completion from quest completion or reward claim.
- Include solo/duo adjustments and recovery rather than assuming six-player instructions transfer.
- Spoilers are allowed; keep lore separate unless it explains the mechanic.
- Cite a real map/video/guide. Do not claim to have watched a video from its title or thumbnail.
- Use fair-play strategies and current mechanics; do not supply automation, anti-cheat bypass, or account-recovery services as gameplay shortcuts.

## Saved guide metadata

Activity/variant, date checked, source update date, exact source section, prerequisites, ordered steps, role/callout convention, failure recovery, rewards, known issues, and unresolved questions. Save only when requested; do not mirror a complete external guide.
