# Farming

Use this file for efficient reward routes.

Last system refresh: 2026-06-16

## Farming Decision Tree

1. What item do I want?
2. Is the source fixed, rotating, or vendor-focused?
3. Can I target the drop directly?
4. Is it solo, matchmade, or fireteam-only?
5. What is the fastest repeatable clear?
6. What difficulty gives the best reward per minute for me?
7. Is there a weekly lockout, rotation, or currency bottleneck?
8. Does score, difficulty, attunement, or activity grade affect the drop?
9. Is there a better live source for the loot table?

## Farm Types

| Farm | Best For | Watchouts |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor focusing | targeted ritual loot | currency cost and rank requirements |
| Weekly featured raid/dungeon | endgame loot | rotation and fireteam needed |
| Portal activities | power and gear progression | score and difficulty efficiency |
| Exotic missions | exotic weapons and catalysts | ownership and mission access |
| Trials | Adept PvP weapons | weekend only, map and reward rotation |
| Iron Banner | IB weapons and armor | every four weeks baseline |
| Monument of Triumph | cosmetics, marks, triumph goals | triumph requirements |
| Pantheon boss rotation | boss-specific rewards | verify weekly featured reprised/encore bosses |
| Attunement | higher odds for selected weapon | check exact source and inventory attunement state |
| Lost sectors | targeted solo farms | daily rotation, Champions, shields, threats |

## Efficient Farming Rules

- Farm the easiest activity that drops the thing you need.
- Do not farm a hard mode slowly if a lower mode gives better rewards per hour.
- Keep one comfortable loadout for repeated clears.
- Track drops in Markdown so you do not farm items you already have.
- If bored, switch farms before mistakes waste time.
- Use the live source before starting a time-consuming farm.
- Stop farming when the marginal upgrade is tiny unless the item is required for a build.

# Weapons

This file is for weapon evaluation, perk priorities, Champion coverage, and personal vault notes.

Last system refresh: 2026-06-16

## Source Priority

For weapon advice, use this order:

1. My personal export or weapon list, if available.
2. Live sheets: Aegis Endgame Analysis for viability, Aegis Boss Damage for DPS, Destiny Data Compendium or Clarity for exact perk behavior.
3. D2Foundry for exact stat, perk-column, and enhanced-perk checking.
4. Light.gg for popularity and database context.
5. Bungie patch notes for official sandbox changes.

Popularity is not the same as strength. Treat Light.gg popularity as context, not proof.

## Quick Keep Rules

Keep or test a weapon when it has at least one of these:

- strong damage perk plus strong reload or ammo perk
- subclass verb synergy
- rare frame or element combo
- strong PvP stat package
- enhanced traits or high gear tier
- raid, dungeon, Trials, Iron Banner, or limited-source origin trait
- personal comfort pick you actually use
- strong Champion coverage for the content you play
- a roll that becomes stronger because the weapon can be tier-upgraded
- a role that is hard to replace, such as debuff, support, blinding, ammo economy, movement, or burst

Shard or deprioritize when:

- it has no damage, utility, or stat identity
- you have a better version in the same slot
- it is only kept because it was hard to get
- the perks fight the weapon's purpose
- it is a PvP roll on a weapon you never use in PvP
- it is low tier, common-source, wrong perks, and not meaningfully upgradable into a role you need
- it duplicates a stronger weapon in the same slot, element, frame, and role

Neutral vault cleanup means do not mass-delete edge cases without checking source, role, and replacement.

## PvE Roll Priorities

| Role | Good Perk Types |
|---|---|
| Add clear primary | reload, damage, elemental verbs, AoE effects |
| Champion control | reliability, range, intrinsic Champion coverage, utility |
| Special burst | ammo economy, burst damage, reload, autoloading |
| Boss DPS heavy | damage rotation perks, ammo efficiency, reload bypass, origin trait |
| Utility weapon | blinding, disorienting, debuffing, support, subclass synergy |
| Solo/duo safety | range, ammo stability, reliable stun, crowd control, self-healing, low-risk uptime |
| Speed/farm weapon | one-shot thresholds, movement support, reload bypass, AoE, low mental load |

## PvE Role Questions

Before judging a PvE weapon, ask:

- Is it for add clear, boss damage, Champion control, burst, utility, or farming?
- Does it work at the activity's real range?
- Does it need precision?
- Does it solve ammo pressure or create more of it?
- Does it pair with the subclass verbs in the build?
- Does the frame provide the needed Anti-Champion effect?
- Is the weapon tier or upgrade path good enough to justify keeping it?

## PvP Roll Priorities

| Weapon Need | Good Perk Types |
|---|---|
| Duel consistency | range, stability, handling, accuracy, target acquisition |
| Opening fight | opening shot, keep away style perks, accuracy boosts |
| Follow-up kills | kill chaining damage perks, reload boosts |
| Special weapons | handling, accuracy, ammo consistency |
| Controller comfort | stability and aim assist matter more |
| Mouse comfort | range and handling often matter more |

For mouse and keyboard, range, handling, accuracy, recoil feel, and first-shot consistency usually beat pure stability unless the weapon needs stability to stay controllable.

## PvP Role Questions

- Is this for Trials, Competitive, Iron Banner, or casual Crucible?
- Is the roll for dueling, laning, cleanup, pick potential, or ability combo support?
- Does the weapon fit the expected map range?
- Does it help the first fight, or only after a kill?
- Is the special weapon plan clear?
- Does it compete with a better archetype in the same slot?

## Gear Tiers

Weapon tiers matter, but perks still matter. A lower-tier weapon with perfect synergy can beat a higher-tier weapon with awkward perks.

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Base weapon, can use seasonal mods once acquired |
| Tier 2 | Enhanced traits |
| Tier 3 | Two traits per column |
| Tier 4 | Enhanced weapon mods and enhanced barrels/magazines |
| Tier 5 | Enhanced origin traits, three traits per column, prestige visual treatment |

Post-Monument note:

- Weapons integrated into Gear Tiering can be upgraded through the weapon inspection screen for a cost.
- Upgrading can unlock enhanced perk benefits and Tier 5 cosmetics.
- Upgrading does not add extra perk choices to a weapon's existing columns, so natural multi-perk drops are still worth chasing.
- Fully Masterworked weapons gain a bonus to all stats equal to the weapon tier.

## Tier Decision Rules

| Situation | Decision |
|---|---|
| Perfect roll, low tier, source is hard | Keep and consider upgrading |
| Good roll, low tier, easy source | Replace later |
| Bad roll, high tier | Test only if the frame/source is rare |
| Great roll, Tier 4 or Tier 5 | Keep unless directly outclassed |
| Crafted raid weapon | Check Relic upgrade path and current perk pool |

# Perk Glossary

This is a practical glossary for judging weapon rolls. It is not exhaustive.

For exact numbers and hidden behavior, check Clarity, Destiny Data Compendium, D2Foundry, or the relevant live spreadsheet before making a final optimization claim.

## PvE Perk Buckets

| Bucket | What It Does | Examples To Look For |
|---|---|---|
| Reload | keeps damage flowing | reload speed, auto reload, overflow, reconstruction-style effects |
| Damage | improves kill speed | direct damage boosts, precision boosts, surrounded-style effects |
| Add clear | clears groups | explosions, elemental effects, chain damage |
| Ammo | improves uptime | ammo generation, reserves behavior, special/heavy economy |
| Utility | controls or debuffs | blinding, disorienting, weakening, slowing, jolting |
| Subclass | feeds a build loop | scorch, volatile, jolt, unravel, freeze, sever, overshield, restoration |
| Debuff | increases team damage or weakens a target | weaken, suppress, scorch/ignition setups, weapon-specific debuffs |
| Rotation | improves boss damage sequences | auto-loading, bait-and-switch style effects, swap bonuses, ready speed |
| Safety | keeps you alive while dealing damage | healing, overshield, damage resist, disorient, slow, freeze |

## PvP Perk Buckets

| Bucket | What It Does |
|---|---|
| Range | improves damage falloff and dueling distance |
| Stability | improves recoil control and controller comfort |
| Handling | improves ready, stow, and ADS speed |
| Accuracy | helps shots land under pressure |
| Targeting | improves aim assist and consistency |
| Kill chaining | helps convert one win into the next fight |
| Airborne / movement | supports aggressive movement or off-angle play |
| Flinch control | helps hold shots through incoming damage |
| Consistency | improves the first duel before any kill-triggered perk activates |

## Evaluation Notes

- Damage perks are not automatically best; check uptime, activity difficulty, and whether enemies live long enough.
- Reload perks are strongest when the weapon cannot reload through an exotic, trait, dodge, rift, or swap loop.
- Utility perks can beat damage perks in GMs, solo dungeons, Trials, or mechanics-heavy encounters.
- PvE add-clear perks lose value if the weapon is mainly for boss damage.
- PvP kill perks are weaker in Trials if they do nothing for the first duel.
- Enhanced perks and tier upgrades matter, but perk synergy comes first.

## Fast Verdict Language

Use these labels when evaluating vault exports:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Keep | clearly useful and should stay |
| Test | promising but needs playtime |
| Replace | usable, but look for a better version |
| Shard | safe to delete unless sentimental |
| Archive | keep for collection, old meta, or unusual trait combo |
| God Roll | best or near-best roll for an important role |
| Niche | keep only for a specific strategy, source, or interaction |

An exotic is lower priority when:

- it only boosts a weak playstyle
- it is strong only in one narrow activity
- it requires perfect play for average payoff
- it conflicts with the activity's range, boss design, or enemy density
- it blocks a stronger exotic weapon or armor choice without solving a clear job

| Champion solution | exotic weapon only if legendary frame coverage is not enough |
| PvP neutral strength | exotic armor unless an exotic weapon is meta-defining |
| solo consistency | exotic armor that keeps the loop alive |

## Current Armor Context

- Armor 3.0 is the current system for new drops after The Edge of Fate.
- Armor stats scale point-by-point instead of only at multiples of 10.
- New armor can have set bonuses at two and four pieces.
- Armor tiers mainly control stat total, with Tier 4 and Tier 5 adding extra benefits.
- Exotic armor is not tiered and generally rolls like Tier 2 armor, but still matters because exotic perks define builds.
- Returning old armor can be a starting point, but higher tiered gear with set bonuses should replace it when it improves the build.

| New Stat | Replaced | Main Value |
|---|---|---|
| Weapons | Mobility | reload, handling, weapon damage scaling, ammo support at high investment |
| Health | Resilience | orb healing, flinch resistance, shield recharge, extra shield value at high investment |
| Class | Recovery | class ability cooldown and class ability energy gains |
| Melee | Strength | melee cooldown and melee damage at high investment |
| Grenade | Discipline | grenade cooldown and grenade damage at high investment |
| Super | Intellect | Super energy from damaging targets and Super damage at high investment |

Important:

- Stats scale point by point instead of only at multiples of 10.
- Stats have a normal benefit from 1 to 100.
- Stats gain an enhanced benefit from 101 to 200.
- Armor 3.0 pieces roll with primary, secondary, and tertiary stats.
- Set bonuses activate at two and four pieces from the same armor set.
- The Weapons stat also influences Special and Heavy ammo generation bar speed.
- Guardian Ranks can unlock systems like loadouts and mods.

## Set Bonus Planning

Armor set bonuses are build pieces, not passive flavor.

| Choice | When To Use |
|---|---|
| 2-piece + 2-piece | when two smaller bonuses solve two different jobs |
| 4-piece | when the full set bonus is central to the build |
| ignore set bonus | only if exotic/stat quality or activity requirement matters more |
| activity-specific legacy armor | use only when the activity mod or legacy benefit is worth losing modern tier/set value |

Ask:

- Does the set bonus increase damage, survival, ability uptime, ammo, mobility, or utility?
- Does it activate reliably in the target activity?
- Does it beat better stat distribution?
- Does it work in PvP, PvE, or both?
- Is this for solo/duo, where survival bonuses may beat team-only utility?

## What To Keep

Keep armor when:

- the archetype matches a build you actually use
- it is Tier 4 or Tier 5
- it has strong primary and secondary stats for a favorite exotic setup
- it completes a useful two-piece or four-piece set bonus
- it fixes a stat gap in an existing loadout
- it is the best archetype for a high-value Hunter or Warlock build
- it enables 101-200 investment in a stat that the build actually uses

Shard or deprioritize armor when:

- low tier and wrong archetype
- no useful set bonus
- duplicates a better piece
- only exists because the total looks high but the stats are wrong

|---|---|---|---|

| Melee loop | Melee | Health | Class or Weapons |
| Super burst | Super | Weapons | Health |
| PvP neutral | Weapons | Health | Class |
| Solo survival | Health | Weapons | Class or loop stat |
| Speed / mobility | Weapons | Class | Health or loop stat |
| Support | Class or Super | Health | Weapons |
| Crowd control | Grenade or Class | Health | Weapons |

## Build Planning Notes

- A build should pick stats based on role, not just total stat number.
- For solo and duo play, Health is usually a required survival floor unless another survival engine fully replaces it.
- Weapons is high value for weapon DPS, PvP, ammo pressure, and general handling.
- Class is high value for Hunter dodge and Warlock rift loops.
- Super matters most when the Super is a major damage or support piece.
- Grenade and Melee matter when the build actually loops through those abilities.
- Armor archetype should match the role before chasing tier.
- For Hunter, Class is often more valuable when dodge uptime drives reload, melee loops, invisibility, or PvP movement.
- For Warlock, Class is valuable when rift, souls, support, or recovery windows are part of the engine.

## Armor Evaluation Process

1. Identify class, slot, set, tier, and archetype.
2. Identify primary, secondary, and tertiary stats.
3. Check whether it supports a real build role.
4. Check whether it completes a two-piece or four-piece set bonus.
5. Compare against existing armor in the same slot.
6. Keep high-tier, correct-archetype pieces even before the final build exists.
7. Mark low-tier wrong-archetype duplicates as cleanup candidates.

# Build Request Protocol

Use this protocol whenever the user asks for a Destiny 2 build.

The goal is to ask enough questions to avoid a generic build, then produce a copy-friendly build card that can be manually recreated in game.

## Rule

Do not create the final build until the required intake fields are answered or confidently inferred from saved preferences.

If a required field is missing, ask concise questions in one short batch. Keep asking follow-up questions only when the answer would materially change the build. If the user explicitly says to assume, proceed and list the assumptions.

## Token-Efficient Intake

Use saved defaults aggressively. Do not ask questions already answered by the prompt, saved defaults, or the user's standing preferences.

Ask only for:

- the activity or mode
- class, if not inferable as Hunter or Warlock
- goal, if DPS/survivability/add clear/PvP/farming is unclear
- gear-access limits, if owned gear matters
- complexity, if the build could be either simple or rotation-heavy
- artifact preference only if a non-PvP build depends on a specific artifact family and the best choice cannot be inferred

Do not ask about Titan unless the user asks for Titan. Do not ask for platform, spoilers, general playstyle, or favorite gear unless the request depends on them.

## Saved Defaults

Use these defaults unless the user says otherwise:

| Field | Default |
|---|---|
| Classes | Hunter or Warlock |
| Fireteam | solo or duo |
| Platform/input | PC mouse and keyboard |
| Style | optimal, numbers-first, current meta |
| Sources | browse live sources for current/meta-sensitive questions |
| Spoilers | allowed |
| Favorites | none; use strongest option |

## Required Intake Fields

Ask for any missing required fields:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Activity | What activity or mode is this for? |
| Class | Hunter or Warlock? |
| Purpose | What should the build optimize: DPS, survivability, add clear, PvP dueling, support, speed, farming, or hybrid? |
| Difficulty | What difficulty or seriousness: casual, endgame, GM, raid, dungeon, Trials, speedrun-level? |
| Fireteam | Solo, duo, or group? |
| Gear access | Should I use only gear you own/exported, or assume access and include replacements? |
| Complexity | Simple loop, advanced rotation, or both? |

If the user already answered these in the prompt, do not ask again.

## Context Questions By Build Type

Ask only the relevant questions after the required fields are known.

### PvE Boss DPS

- Which boss or encounter?
- Is the damage phase short, long, or unknown?
- Is the boss crit-friendly?
- Is the team already covering buffs/debuffs?
- Are swaps acceptable?
- Do you want speedrun-level damage or realistic consistency?
- Any locked loadout, surge, threat, or modifier?

### Solo / Duo Endgame

- What usually kills you: boss damage, adds, Champions, mechanics, ammo, or panic movement?
- Do you want safer consistency or faster clears?
- Are you comfortable with invisibility, Devour, restoration, or crowd-control loops?
- Do you need all Champion coverage yourself?

### PvP

- Is this for Trials, Competitive, Iron Banner, Control, or general Crucible?
- Preferred range: close, mid, long, or map-dependent?
- Do you want primary-duel strength, special-weapon picks, ability pressure, or shutdown tools?
- Any weapon archetype you want to force or avoid?
- Is this for solo queue or duo?

### Farming

- What reward are you farming?
- Do you want fastest clears, lowest effort, or best reward quality?
- Is the activity solo, duo, matchmade, or fireteam-only?
- Should the build reduce attention/fatigue?

### Raid / Dungeon Mechanics

- Are you doing mechanics, add clear, boss DPS, runner, or support?
- Do you need movement tech?
- Do you need revive safety?
- Is the loadout locked?
- Is this a first clear, farm, solo, flawless, or speed goal?

## Build Creation Steps

1. Confirm the intake.
2. Check local notes.
3. For Compendium-style values, check `D:\Coding\DestinyDataCompendium\Destiny_Data_Compendium.txt` before live sheets.
4. Check live sources only if current data matters or the local export does not answer the needed value.
5. Pick the build role.
6. Pick the class/subclass shell.
7. Pick exotic armor and decide whether an exotic weapon is worth the slot.
8. Pick weapons and Champion coverage.
9. Pick Armor 3.0 stats and archetypes.
10. Add set bonus and mod plan.
11. Add artifact, exact artifact mod picks, and activity modifier notes. For every non-PvP build, separate mandatory artifact picks from flex picks and explain what the artifact layer is doing.
12. Write the simple loop.
13. Write advanced rotation if requested or useful.
14. Add substitutions and farm priorities.
15. Provide a final copy-friendly card.

## Stop Conditions

Ask another question if:

- activity is unknown
- class is unknown
- build purpose is unknown
- gear access is unknown and the build depends on specific exotics or weapons
- PvP/PvE context changes the build
- boss or encounter details determine the DPS setup
- non-PvP artifact choice cannot be safely inferred and materially changes the build

Proceed if:

- all required fields are answered
- missing details can be safely inferred from saved defaults
- user explicitly says to assume

## Output Discipline

Lead with the build card or the verdict. Keep source explanation short unless the user asks for the math trail.

For high-end or DPS builds, include only the numbers and rotation details that change the decision. Avoid listing every viable alternative; give the best pick, one easier substitute, and one farm priority when useful.

# Buildcrafting Framework

Last researched: 2026-06-16

This file defines what a Destiny 2 build means in this workspace and how to reason about build types, priorities, questions, and output.

## What A Build Is

A build is a complete combat plan, not just an exotic and subclass.

A complete build answers:

- What activity is this for?
- What is the win condition?
- What keeps me alive?
- What clears weak enemies?
- What kills majors, Champions, and bosses?
- What weapons cover the ranges and Champion types?
- What stats, armor archetypes, set bonuses, and mods support the loop?
- What artifact choices or live modifiers change the setup?
- What is the simple loop?
- What is the advanced rotation when optimizing hard?
- What swaps or alternatives are worth using?

## Build Types

Most builds are hybrids, but every build should have a primary purpose.

| Build Type | Primary Purpose | Typical Priorities | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boss DPS | maximize damage in a phase | heavy/special rotation, Super, buffs/debuffs, reload bypass, ammo, swap speed | raid boss, dungeon boss, Pantheon |
| Burst Damage | delete one target quickly | Super burst, exotic weapon burst, short rotation, safe setup window | Champion, miniboss, short boss phase |
| Sustained Damage | keep strong damage over long windows | total damage, ammo economy, reload loop, survivability | long dungeon boss, GM boss |
| Add Clear | remove groups fast | AoE perks, subclass verbs, ability uptime, ammo sustain | farming, raids, Arena Ops |
| Survivability | reduce deaths and stabilize mistakes | Health, healing, invisibility, Devour, restoration, overshields, resist, crowd control | solo dungeon, GM, duo play |
| Crowd Control | stop enemies from acting | freeze, suspend, blind, disorient, suppression, slow, ignition chaining | Champion rooms, hard add waves |
| Support | make the team stronger or safer | healing, buffs, debuffs, ammo, revives, team damage windows | raids, dungeons, duo |
| Mobility / Speed | move faster or skip downtime | movement tech, speed buffs, lightweight weapons, grapple, eager-edge style swaps | speedrun, traversal, farming |
| Objective / Mechanics | complete mechanics safely | survival, movement, simple weapons, range, invisibility, low complexity | raid runner, dunker, plate holder |
| PvP Neutral | win ordinary duels | Weapons, Health, Class, handling, range, ability cooldowns, radar discipline | Crucible, Trials |
| PvP Pick / Burst | create first kill or swing fight | sniper/fusion/shotgun plan, shutdown Super, movement, ability combo | Trials, Competitive |
| Farming / Low-Friction | clear fast with low mental load | simple loop, stable ammo, forgiving survival, minimal swaps | repeat activities |

## Build Components

| Component | Decisions To Make |
|---|---|
| Activity context | PvE/PvP, activity, encounter, difficulty, fireteam size, locked loadout, modifiers, reward goal |
| Role | DPS, add clear, support, survival, speed, mechanics, PvP duel, hybrid |
| Class | Hunter or Warlock by default; Titan only when asked |
| Subclass | Solar, Arc, Void, Stasis, Strand, Prismatic |
| Abilities | Super, class ability, movement, melee, grenade |
| Aspects | main verb engine and build identity |
| Fragments | stat tuning, verb access, loop support, damage/survival modifiers |
| Exotic armor | centerpiece, survivability engine, DPS tool, support tool, or neutral PvP tool |
| Exotic weapon | only if it beats exotic armor value or solves a job uniquely |
| Weapons | add clear, priority damage, boss damage, Champion counters, range, ammo, PvP dueling |

## Armor 3.0 Priority Logic

Current Armor 3.0 stats scale point-by-point. They have a main benefit from 1-100 and an enhanced benefit from 101-200.

| Stat | Build Meaning |
|---|---|
| Weapons | gunplay, reload/handling, weapon damage scaling, ammo support, PvP weapon pressure |
| Health | survival, orb healing, flinch resistance, faster shields, extra combatant shield at high investment |
| Class | class ability uptime, class energy scaling, high-investment overshield on class ability |
| Melee | melee ability uptime and high-investment melee damage |
| Grenade | grenade uptime and high-investment grenade damage |
| Super | Super energy from damage and high-investment Super damage |

## Armor Archetype Use

| Archetype | Best For |
|---|---|
| Paragon: Super + Melee | Super burst builds, melee-Super hybrids |
| Grenadier: Grenade + Super | grenade loops, damage Supers, ability DPS |
| Specialist: Class + Weapons | dodge/rift loops, PvP neutral game, weapon-centric builds |
| Brawler: Melee + Health | melee builds that need survival |
| Bulwark: Health + Class | defensive PvE, solo play, PvP survivability, objective roles |

## Priority Order

Use this order when creating a build:

1. Identify activity and win condition.
2. Set the role: DPS, survival, add clear, support, PvP, speed, or hybrid.
3. Check current sources if meta, DPS, live rotations, or sandbox details matter.
4. Pick class and subclass shell.
5. Pick the exotic centerpiece.
6. Solve survivability floor.
7. Solve damage profile.
8. Solve add clear and Champion coverage.
9. Pick weapons and backups.
10. Set Armor 3.0 stat priorities and ideal archetypes.
11. Add set bonus plan.
12. Add mods and artifact picks.
13. Write simple loop.
14. Write advanced rotation if the request rewards optimization.
15. Add substitutions and farm targets.
16. Validate the build against the original activity constraints.

## Survivability Floor

Every PvE build needs a survival floor before damage optimization.

Good survival tools:

- Health investment
- invisibility
- Devour
- restoration
- cure
- overshield
- woven mail
- freeze, suspend, blind, suppression, disorient
- damage resistance
- ranged play
- revive safety
- ammo stability so the build does not collapse mid-fight

For solo and duo play, the build should not assume a teammate will cover healing, revives, or Champion counters.

## Damage Profile

Damage plans must match the encounter.

| Damage Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Burst or sustained? | short phases reward burst, long phases reward total damage and ammo economy |
| Boss range? | determines rockets, GLs, linears, snipers, swords, or machine guns |
| Crit or non-crit? | determines precision weapons vs explosive options |
| Movement during DPS? | stationary rotations differ from moving bosses |
| Add pressure during DPS? | affects survivability and reload choices |
| Buff/debuff already covered? | prevents wasted duplicate support |
| Swap allowed and practical? | affects high-end rotations |
| Ammo starvation likely? | changes weapon and mod picks |

## PvP Build Priorities

PvP builds should prioritize:

1. Weapon consistency.
2. Health and flinch resistance.
3. Class ability uptime.
4. Movement and engagement control.
5. Special weapon plan.
6. Super or shutdown plan.
7. Ability combo only if it reliably wins fights.

For mouse and keyboard, value range, handling, accuracy, and movement control. Stability still matters when it improves consistency, but do not over-prioritize it over range/handling without a weapon-specific reason.
