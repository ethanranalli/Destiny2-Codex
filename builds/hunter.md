# Hunter Candidate Map

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Hunter is the saved main. These are mechanic-based research anchors, not complete builds or a September tier list. Check current entries and compare alternatives for the actual activity.

| Subclass / role | Candidate anchors to investigate | Required checks |
|---|---|---|
| Solar / precision burst | Celestial Nighthawk, Star-Eater Scales | Exact Super, crit access, orb setup, survival, current boss window |
| Void / invisibility and revives | Graviton Forfeit, Omnioculus | Invisibility loop, solo versus allies, survival when revealed |
| Void / weapon-driven clear | Gyrfalcon's Hauberk | Void weapon and current exit-invisibility/volatile triggers |
| Arc / melee or ability pressure | Liar's Handshake, Assassin's Cowl, Gifted Conviction, Shinobu's Vow | Actual ability trigger, close-range safety, kill dependence |
| Stasis / control | Renewal Grasps | Current grenade, Frost Armor/control behavior, energy economy |
| Strand / control or mobility | Cyrtarachne's Facade, The Sixth Coyote | Grapple/dodge/aspect compatibility, suspend versus damage role |
| Prismatic / mixed engines | Relativism with an exact legal pair, or compatible standard exotic | Owned Spirit roll, Prismatic pool, Transcendence, Super-element dependencies |
| PvP / neutral and objectives | Compare current movement, handling, dodge, or ability exotics against the forced weapon | Playlist rules, map range, first-duel value, current breakpoints |

Do not insert Relativism into the pure-element rows. Do not assume every standard exotic improves every Prismatic ability.

Dodge value depends on its actual interaction: reload, melee return, invisibility, positioning, or other trigger. Decide stats from that engine, not an automatic Class-first rule.

For solo/duo, include survival outside invisibility or kill chains and a complete boss/ammo plan. For damage, compare current measured rotations; the frozen-boss Nighthawk exploit was fixed in 9.7.0.1.

Use [intake](build-request-protocol.md), [artifacts](artifacts.md), and [complete output](build-output-template.md). Owned exotic rolls, armor, catalysts, and unlocks remain unknown until imported or stated.
