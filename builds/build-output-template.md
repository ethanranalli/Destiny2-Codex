## Play the build

1. Opening: establish buffs, charges, position, and first ability trigger.
2. Normal loop: exact ability/weapon order and upkeep.
3. Priority target/boss: damage sequence and when to stop/reload/rebuild.
4. Emergency: recovery when kills, ammo, buffs, or ability charges are missing.

For advanced DPS give setup duration, preconditions, cast/swap/reload order, phase/window, ammo consumed, and repeat/fallback. For PvP give first duel, disengage, re-engage, special use, and objective/Super plan.
