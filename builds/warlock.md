# Warlock Candidate Map

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Warlock is the saved secondary. These are research anchors, not ready-to-equip or universally ranked builds.

| Subclass / role | Candidate anchors to investigate | Required checks |
|---|---|---|
| Solar / healing and support | Speaker's Sight, Phoenix Protocol, Sanguine Alchemy | Exact healing/rift/Super trigger, team overlap, own damage |
| Solar / grenade loops | Sunbracers, Starfire Protocol | Actual grenade/melee/rift restrictions, kill dependence, current cooldown returns |
| Void / Devour and grenades | Nezarec's Sin, Contraverse Hold | Current ability/kill triggers and ability energy |
| Void / Child or class ability | Briarbinds, Secant Filaments | Child/rift management, survival against tough targets |
| Arc / ability economy | Fallen Sunstar, Crown of Tempests, Getaway Artist | Ionic Trace/ability prerequisites, current grenade/Arc Soul rules |
| Stasis / control | Osmiomancy Gloves, Rime-Coat Raiment, Ballidorse Wrathweavers | Selected grenade, turret/Super requirements, freeze and damage role |
| Strand / suspend or Threadlings | Mataiodoxia, Swarmers | Arcane Needle/Threadling/Tangle trigger and current verb behavior |
| Prismatic / combined engines | Solipsism with an exact legal pair, or compatible standard exotic | Exact Spirit effects, available abilities, Transcendence and Super element |
| PvP / neutral, movement, lanes | Compare current movement/handling/rift/Blink candidates | First-duel reliability, map range, health/weapon model, mode |

Do not insert Solipsism into pure Solar, Void, Arc, Stasis, or Strand builds. A Prismatic build cannot borrow every ability/aspect from those full subclasses.

Warlock support must still provide damage and mechanics value. If the team already supplies a buff or healing role, compare the marginal benefit of adding another support tool.

Survival through Devour or ability kills needs a non-kill emergency plan. A turret build needs control and ammo outside its strongest cooldown cycle.

Use [intake](build-request-protocol.md), [damage reasoning](damage-and-survival.md), [artifacts](artifacts.md), and [complete output](build-output-template.md). No owned armor or Solipsism export is available yet.
