# Questions To Ask Later

Use this as a checklist for improving the helper after personal data is added.

Answered baseline:

- Main classes: Hunter and Warlock.
- Activity mix: PvE and PvP, broad mix.
- Fireteam: solo or duo with one friend.
- Optimization style: numbers-first, current meta, speedrun-style damage and survivability when useful.
- Input/platform: mouse and keyboard on Epic Games.
- Spoilers: allowed.
- Favorites: no fixed favorite weapons, subclasses, or exotics.

## Account And Playstyle

- Which Hunter exotics are actually owned and high-stat?
- Which Warlock exotics are actually owned and high-stat?
- Which Relativism and Solipsism rolls are owned?
- Which activities feel hardest right now?
- Which PvP range bands feel best on mouse and keyboard?

## Data To Import

- Do I have a current vault export?
- Do I have weapon roll ratings?
- Do I have armor stat exports?
- Do I have an exotic catalyst checklist?
- Do I have a crafted weapon list?
- Do I have raid and dungeon clear history?
- Do I have Trials/Iron Banner weapon rolls to evaluate?
- Do I have DIM tags or notes that should be treated as personal preference?

## Cleanup Questions

- Which weapons are duplicates?
- Which rolls are PvE-only?
- Which rolls are PvP-only?
- Which old weapons are still sentimental?
- Which armor pieces are low-tier and wrong archetype?
- Which exotics need better rolls?

## Build Questions

- What is my best solo build per class?
- What is my best raid DPS build per class?
- What is my safest dungeon build?
- What is my easiest farming loadout?
- What build should I use when tired and just clearing chores?
- What is my best PvP neutral build for Hunter?
- What is my best PvP neutral build for Warlock?
- What high-end swap/DPS rotations are realistic with my actual vault?
