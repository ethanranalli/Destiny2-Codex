## Vault Philosophy

Default rules: neutral cleanup, with separate PvE and PvP verdicts when discussing weapons.

- keep what is useful
- keep what is rare and hard to reacquire
- test strong rolls before deleting
- delete duplicates when one is clearly better
- do not hoard weapons for a meta that may never come back
- use labels like `Keep`, `Shard`, `Test`, `God Roll`, `Niche`, `Replace`, and `Archive`
- favor current data and tested performance over popularity when sources conflict
