# Monument, Rewards Passes, and Retired Events

Reviewed: 2026-09-06. Use [canonical baseline](../00-start-here/source-notes.md) rather than repeating a fixed "latest patch."

The checked [Monument support guide](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/49848696022548-Destiny-2-Monument-of-Triumph-and-Update-9-7-0-Support-Guide) covers ongoing Monument access, rewards-pass changes, and retired seasonal events.

## Keep systems separate

| System | Clarify |
|---|---|
| Monument of Triumph | Tower on-ramp, triumph goals, Legendary Marks and associated rewards |
| Monument to Seasons Past | Archived rewards-pass eligibility, currency and ownership |
| Monument to Lost Lights | Exact exotic acquisition offer and ownership/cost requirements |
| Triumphant Rewards Pass | Current free/premium reward conditions and claim progress |
| Eververse | Exact live item, currency, price, and offer window |
| Retired events | Historical triumph/ornament/source versus currently obtainable replacements |

Do not assume buying or accessing an archived reward pass restores the old seasonal missions. Do not schedule Guardian Games, Solstice, Festival of the Lost, or Dawning from an old live-service calendar when the checked support state retires them.

## Guide output

Start from the exact reward or objective. State what to visit, what prerequisite to finish, currency/ownership needs, and whether the item is still obtainable. Verify live vendor state before recommending spending.

The [Monument FAQ](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/50100493304468--13-Monument-of-Triumph-FAQ) directs the on-ramp through the Tower Monument. Returning-player details belong in [new and returning](new-and-returning.md).
