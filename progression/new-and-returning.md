# New and Returning Player Route

Reviewed: 2026-09-06.

Ask only what changes the next step: new versus returning, class, owned content, exact blocked unlock, and immediate goal. Do not require a complete account questionnaire.

## Practical order

1. Establish access: character, current quest step, owned content, and any login/update issue.
2. For a new character, follow A Guardian Rises/New Light and the actual displayed objectives. For a returning player, skip already completed onboarding.
3. Use the Tower Monument on-ramp if the user has not started it.
4. Unlock the subclass/abilities and essential gear required by the chosen goal.
5. Assemble one legal, obtainable build with a survival loop and the relevant artifact.
6. Improve Power/gear only where the target activity's actual requirements justify it.
7. Choose one progression/farm target that makes the biggest difference; defer marginal min-maxing.
8. Add dungeons/raids/PvP goals with suitable mechanics preparation and team access.

This is a recommendation order, not a mandatory quest chain.

Use [Bungie's new-player route](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/45297505570580--4-A-Path-for-New-Players) for current story/navigation context and the [Monument FAQ](https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/50100493304468--13-Monument-of-Triumph-FAQ) for returner questions. A page's recommended story order is not a content-ownership guarantee.

Separate account Power, item Power, artifact effects, gear tier, armor stats, and activity scaling. Higher Power or tier does not automatically make a better build. Verify exact caps/requirements from current evidence before prescribing a grind.

For campaign order, tailor chronological versus fastest-unlock routes to the user. Spoilers are allowed. Clearly identify vaulted/nonplayable story and point to a lore summary instead of a nonexistent mission.
