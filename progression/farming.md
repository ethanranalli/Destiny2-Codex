# Efficient Farming

Reviewed: 2026-09-06.

Lead with the fastest repeatable route that can actually reward the target under the user's constraints.

## Decision order

1. Exact desired item, version, roll, tier, pattern, catalyst, material, or triumph.
2. Ownership/unlock eligibility and current source.
3. Targeting, focusing, attunement, encounter pool, and difficulty/grade requirements.
4. Repeatability and per-character/account/weekly restrictions for that specific reward.
5. Solo/duo/matchmade/team practicality.
6. Real completion time including setup, travel, loading, resets, wipes, and currency cost.
7. Stop condition: useful roll, completed pattern/catalyst, budget, or diminishing returns.

Use current [loot tables and guides](../00-start-here/community-guides.md), official reward changes, and the exact live activity card. Check rotation for the actual reset window. Do not assume old Lost Sector exotic armor slots, one featured Trials weapon, or unlimited drops for every reward.

## Compare routes

Eligible useful rewards per hour is more informative than nominal drop quality. If drop probability and expected reward count are known, include them; otherwise compare observed clear times and targeting without inventing odds.

Arithmetic example only: a repeatable route with a 20% independent success chance would take five attempts on average, not guarantee success by attempt five. Destiny loot can include protection, lockouts, multiple pools, or unknown probabilities; use its actual rules.

| Route | Target/eligibility | Fireteam | Full cycle time | Targeting/lockout | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Choose a loadout that sustains repeated clears. Include a low-effort fallback if complex swaps or reset failures lose the advantage.

## Practical priorities

- Build-enabling exotic/catalyst/unlock before a tiny stat improvement.
- A useful owned roll can be cheaper to upgrade than replacing it; verify upgrade eligibility.
- Featured raid/dungeon farming requires exact encounter and reward rules.
- Vendor focusing depends on present cost, unlock state, and currency budget.
- Class item farming must target a legal pair; attunement does not guarantee the other perk.
- A ghost/Armorer selection can help an armor chase only when verified for that source.

Never perform dismantling, spending, or account actions merely because this file recommends them.
