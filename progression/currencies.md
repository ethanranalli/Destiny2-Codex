# Currencies, Focusing, and Upgrade Decisions

Reviewed: 2026-09-06.

Use the user's current budget and the vendor's current costs. Separate a recommendation from making a purchase or spending materials.

## Roles

| Currency/material | Common decision |
|---|---|
| Glimmer | Focusing and upgrades versus immediate needs |
| Enhancement Cores / Prisms | Build completion and upgrade efficiency |
| Ascendant Shards | High-value armor upgrades |
| Ascendant Alloy | Relevant crafting/enhancement costs |
| Exotic Ciphers | Eligible acquisition/focusing options |
| Spoils of Conquest | Raid acquisition/focusing and applicable legacy armor |
| Strange Coins | Xur rewards, catalysts, or useful inventory |
| Bright Dust | Wanted cosmetics and limited offers |
| Chronologs | Eligible archived reward/catch-up options |
| Legendary Marks / activity-specific ciphers | Monument or activity reward rules; verify exact spend |

Do not infer present caps or costs from an old spreadsheet. The [9.7.0 economy changes](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/destiny_update_9_7_0) and later fixes supersede earlier values. Avoid obsolete Legendary Shard/Planetary Material farm advice.

## Spend priority

1. Unlock or complete a build component that changes capability.
2. Upgrade a verified useful roll that is actually used.
3. Fill a clear armor energy/stat/set gap.
4. Chase a modest improvement only when the expected gain beats the cost.
5. Keep cosmetic/collection priorities separate from combat strength.

For vendor advice identify item/version, selected roll, tier/upgrade path, total cost, unlock condition, owned alternative, and time window.

Vendor roles may include Zavala, Shaxx, Saint-14, Saladin, Drifter, Banshee-44, Xur, Ada-1, Rahool, and Hawthorne. Verify the actual offer; naming the vendor does not establish inventory.
